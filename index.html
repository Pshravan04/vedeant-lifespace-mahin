About Raymond Mahim Bay Sea View

Raymond Mahim Bay Sea View is a premium residential project located in the heart of Mumbai. Designed to offer modern living, it stands tall with 31 habitable floors set across a 2-acre low-density estate. The project provides spacious 3 & 4 BHK homes with expansive carpet areas, ensuring maximum comfort for families. With exclusive podium and basement amenities, an open-to-sky rooftop, and uninterrupted Arabian Sea views, this project brings a perfect balance of lifestyle and sophistication, making it an ideal choice for homebuyers in Mumbai.

Crafted by Raymond Realty, a name known for trust, quality, and timeless excellence. Strategically located in Mahim, it is the perfect address for those seeking an elevated standard of living.
