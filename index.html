About Raymond Mahim Bay Sea View

Raymond Mahim Bay Sea View stands as an architectural beacon of contemporary luxury on the historic Mahim Causeway. Set across a serene 2-acre private estate with 31 habitable storeys, it offers a tranquil coastal sanctuary right at the crossroads of South Mumbai and Bandra-Kurla Complex.

Each residence has been oriented to capture natural maritime cross-breezes and endless views of the Arabian Sea. Crafted with Raymond Realty’s hallmark legacy of precision engineering, thoughtful spatial design, and uncompromising quality.
